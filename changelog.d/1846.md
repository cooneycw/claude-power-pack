### Changed

- The issue contract (`docs/agents/issue-contract.md`) gains "Supported scope and
  proportional prevention": approve the supported scope before choosing
  safeguards, justify a new permanent gate beyond a reproducible defect, and let
  a valid finding end as an **accepted limitation** on the approver's ruling,
  with no fix promised and no successor issue. It is a disposition for a
  finding; the three delivery dispositions stay three.
- `/flow:auto` Step 6 triage, `/codex:auto` Step 5 and the declined-pass policy
  in `docs/agents/counter-model-evidence.md` each offer that route and link the
  contract. The receipt counts an accepted limitation under `deferred`, and the
  PR's review block names it in words.
- `/codex:code_review` tells the reviewer to judge a change against its approved
  scope and to label a concern outside it as a proposed scope expansion. Such a
  concern is still reported.
