### Fixed

- `check-doc-helper-flags` reads every command with one shell reader instead
  of three that disagreed. A flag written after the helper's own `$(...)`
  closes is no longer blamed on the helper, a quoted argument continued onto
  the next line stays quoted, and a `(` inside quotes within a substitution no
  longer hides the flags after it. The reader also follows `<(...)` and
  `>(...)`, a comment straight after `;`, escaped backticks, and
  `--name=value$(...)` options. A helper path inside a shell comment is
  counted in its own note instead of being checked as an invocation. One
  control case per shape.
