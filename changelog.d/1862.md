### Changed

- A fix accepted from counter-model review that touches only the issue's plan
  record (`docs/flow-runs/issue-<N>.md`, its `.as-read.md` snapshot and its
  `.lanes-demonstrated.md` rows) no longer forces another review pass.
  `counter-model-receipt.py write` binds the reviewed head and records the
  move in `reviewed_artifact.plan_record_move`; any other path after the
  reviewed head is still refused and named. The receipt's `head` keeps its
  meaning, so the enrolment check and consumer CI read it as before.
